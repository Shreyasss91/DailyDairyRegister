Imports

A source file is read, judged and staged. Nothing reaches the master data until you accept the batch, and a batch with an unusable row cannot be accepted. Read and accept the areas and the feeders before the DTC roster. A roster is refused until both are stored, and refused if it names a feeder the feeder master does not carry: a transformer resolves to a feeder only through that master, and a roster read first would leave every transformer unassigned.

Leave records

A leave record is one date and one leave type. Reading a monthly leave sheet writes one record per date in each row's range, and replaces what was stored on the dates the sheet names. A leave the sheet records as rejected, cancelled or withdrawn is left out.

Generate a draft register

The result is an allocator-backed draft. It is not verified work and it is not a reviewed register. Regular activities are the approved catalogue's work, recorded on every working day: the minimum is the floor a day never falls below and the maximum the ceiling it never exceeds, so the day's own count is drawn between them. Set both to 0 to leave the days to field visits only.

Each kind of visit has its own ceiling on one working day, so a day of transformer checks does not spend the allowance for areas or feeders. They are independent: a date may hold up to each of them. The activity minimum is the only minimum here - every working day records at least that many approved activities - and the safety meeting recorded on Mondays is not counted against either number.

Backup and restore

A backup holds the whole database: the masters, the staged imports, every revision, every review and lock, every closed month and every version issued from it. Keep the downloaded file somewhere other than this computer as well. Restoring replaces the database in use with the one inside the file.

Take a backup

The copy is taken while the tool keeps working, and it holds every change that had been saved when it started.

Restore from a backup

Choose a backup file and check it first. Nothing is replaced until you confirm, and a copy of the database in use is kept beside it before anything changes.

Start a new office

One installation holds one office. Handing this one to a different office means removing every record the present office entered: the masters, the staged imports, every draft, every review and lock, every closed month and every version issued from it. Nothing is removed until you type the word below, a backup of everything is taken first, and a copy of the database as it stands is kept beside it afterwards.

Clear everything, including the calendar

Everything goes, including the General Holiday list and the weekly-off rules. The tool then behaves exactly as it did on its first start.

Clear everything, but keep the calendar

The General Holiday list, the leave types and the weekly-off rules stay, because they are the same for any office in the state; every other record goes.
